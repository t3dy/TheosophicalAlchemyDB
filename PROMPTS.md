## One-Sentence Vision

A **concept-first, scholarly encyclopedia of Rosicrucian and theosophical-alchemical traditions (16th–18th c.)** that serves scholars, interested readers, and practitioners with relational browsing, rich provenance, and rigorous historiographical discipline.

---

## The Problem We're Solving

Rosicrucian and theosophical-alchemical scholarship is scattered across:
- Scattered PDF corpora (no unified reference)
- Mixed historiographical frames (legendary vs. historical; mystical vs. scientific)
- No concept-first organization (texts and biographies exist, but concepts don't navigate)
- Missing emblem scholarship (emblem books are illustrations, not entities)
- Inaccessible to non-specialists (jargon overload)

**This portal fixes it.** A single, navigable, rigorously sourced reference that organizes knowledge by *idea*, not *author*.

---

## Core Principles (Non-Negotiable)

### 1. Concept-First Organization ✅ ACTIVE
Users navigate by **philosophical/alchemical idea**, not chronology or author:
- "What is *nigredo*?" → nigredo page → linked concepts, texts, figures, emblems
- "What is *theosis*?" → theosis page with Rosicrucian interpretations
- "What is *hieros gamos*?" → alchemy + Kabbalah + Rosicrucian contextualization
**Current state:** 60 concepts defined; all linked to ≥2 emblems in Phase 2

### 2. Historiographical Rigor (Yates/Vickers Model) ✅ ACTIVE
- **Present scholarly debates fairly:** Don't resolve them artificially; show multiple frames
- **Actor/Analyst distinction:** What Rosicrucians called themselves ≠ what modern scholars call them
- **No anachronism:** Use period-appropriate terminology and frameworks
- **No speculative content:** All claims are source-trackable; citation included
- **Gender awareness:** Explicitly note women's participation and obstacles in transmission
**Current state:** All Phase 1 entries meet this standard; Vickers critique integrated

### 3. Emblems as Philosophical Instruments (NOT Illustrations) ✅ ACTIVE
Each emblem is a **navigable, researched entity** with:
- Visual description (200-300 words, Szulakowska framework: composition, symbolism, context)
- Scholarly essay (500-800 words) with art-historical analysis
- Alchemical & mystical interpretation (grounded in primary sources)
- Image (extracted from PDF or public-domain) with provenance
- Links to concepts (what does it illustrate?), figures (who created it?), texts (where discussed?)
- Scholarly apparatus (De Jong-style source citations, debates)
**Current state:** 30 emblems created Phase 1; expanding to 100+ Phase 2

### 4. Relational Browsing (Every Entity ≥3 Links) ✅ ACTIVE
- A figure biography links to influenced figures, key concepts, major texts, related emblems
- A concept page links to related concepts, texts, figures, emblems
- A text summary links to author, concepts developed, related works, emblem references
- Every emblem links to concepts, figures, texts, related emblems
**Current state:** All 206 entities meet minimum; many have 5+ links

### 5. Embodied Knowledge & Transmission Genealogy ✅ ACTIVE
Following Mike Zuber's *Spiritual Alchemy* and Joscelyn Godwin's genealogical approach:
- Ground concepts in actual practice/operations (what practitioners actually DO)
- Show transmission: who learned from whom, what was lost/recovered
- Track continental/cultural differences (Islamic alchemy ≠ European; medieval ≠ Renaissance)
- Document women's participation despite historical marginalization
- Show how ideas spread across cultures/centuries
**Current state:** All Phase 1 entries include transmission genealogy and gender awareness

### 6. Academic Voice (Learned, Rigorous, Respectful) ✅ ACTIVE
- No mysticism, no New Age framing, no channeled content
- Clear, accessible language (complex ideas, simple words)
- Learned but not pedantic; rigorous but not defensive; respectful but not reverent
- Explain jargon on first use
- Cite sources consistently with page numbers
- Mark all AI-generated content with review status
**Current state:** STYLEGUIDE_UPDATED.md establishes voice standards; all entries meet them

## Anti-Patterns (What We Will NOT Do)

❌ Mystical language or New Age framing
❌ Speculative content without source attribution
❌ Conflating scholar voice with practitioner voice
❌ Treating legendary Rosicrucian "orders" as historical fact
❌ Alchemy as purely chemical or purely symbolic (present the spectrum)
❌ Hallucinated scholarship or fictional citations
❌ AI-generated content without review or draft-status marking
❌ Orphan pages (every page links to ≥3 others)
❌ Anachronistic terminology (use period-appropriate language)

## The Emblem Scholar's North Star

For emblem work specifically:

- **Emblems are NOT illustrations**; they are *researched entities*
- Every emblem has: image, provenance, Maier/Micrelius text, scholarly interpretation, linked concepts
- De Jong's *Atalanta Fugiens* scholarship is our anchor; other emblem book traditions supplement
- Visual analysis is rigorous, not intuitive (describe what's *visible* in the plate, not what it *means*)
- Every emblem links to ≥2 concepts, ≥1 text, ≥1 figure
- All emblem images must be public domain or openly licensed (no rights-restricted images)
