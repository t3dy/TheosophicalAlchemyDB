KEY THEMES IN EMBLEMS 1-20:

I. FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES (Emblems 1-2)
   - Universal Correspondence (Hermetic axiom: above ↔ below)
   - Natural Analogy (embryo development mirrors Stone generation)
   - Source: Tabula Smaragdina (Hermes Trismegistus)

II. PURIFICATION & WHITENING (Emblems 3-6)
   - Nigredo → Albedo transition (black → white)
   - Washing, calcination, dissolution
   - Color symbolism: black (corruption) to white (purity)
   - Source: women's domestic work as teaching model

III. CRITICAL MIDDLE STAGES (Emblems 7-15)
   - Conjunction (sacred marriage of opposites)
   - Putrefaction as generation (death → rebirth)
   - Sealed vessel and hidden nourishment
   - Equilibrium and precise measurement
   - The potter's wheel: circulatio principle

IV. FINAL PERFECTION (Emblems 16-20)
   - Self-nourishment (ouroboros principle)
   - Four kinds of fire (graduated heating)
   - Incombustibility (transcendent nature)
   - Rubedo (red perfection, kingship)
   - Cauda Pavonis (peacock's tail, polychromatic perfection)
