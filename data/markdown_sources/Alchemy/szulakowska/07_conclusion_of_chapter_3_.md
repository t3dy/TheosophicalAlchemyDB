Conclusion of Chapter 3:

metaphor for the alchemical process, symbolizing the suffering and sacrifice that were

their own esoteric ideas. The chapter highlights the role of sacrifice in alchemical practice,

illustrations, Szulakowska shows how the crucified body of Christ became a central symbol for
