## Section: Conclusion

Conclusion
Szulakowska’s study reveals the profound impact that Kabbalistic thought had on early modern
alchemical practices and Christian theosophy. By integrating Kabbalistic cosmology into their
frameworks, figures like Boehme, Paracelsus, and Fludd were able to bridge the gap between
esoteric knowledge and Christian theology, offering a mystical interpretation of the divine plan
for creation, redemption, and the ultimate healing of the cosmos.
