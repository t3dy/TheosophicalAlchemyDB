The question remains as to the degree of Union with the divine. What Khunrath
describes sounds far beyond the intermediary uniting with God through grace
and the traditional rituals of the Church. But is the end-state a direct encounter
with God, as other, in the depths of the soul or a union without distinction?1023
At times it appears to be the former, whether presented as an elevated rela-
tionship with God, kinship with Christ, or friendship with the Holy Spirit, that
somehow preserves the distinction between Creator and creature:1024
Then IEHOVAH, the Father; IHSVH CHRIST crucified, the Brother; HOLY
SPIRIT, the Friend, will enter into you, will dwell and be seated, King,
in his Micro-Cosmic palace or throne, YOURSELF, tri-une, reborn, truly
catholic, as in the second figure of this Amphitheatre. Transmuted into
[something] better by penitential Washing, Isaiah 1:16, you will join
and unite with GOD, you will be illumined by a ray of the superceles-
tial Sun.1025

A passage in the Amphitheatre, from the start of the section on Conjunction
and Union, deserves to be considered in more depth here:
The Reduction to the Simplicity of the Monad […] is the Method or way,
by which MAN can and must be regenerated, reinstated in integrity,
Divinely brought back to GOD whom he deserted by his own dissension,
to be made a new man, conjoined, illumined and united with GOD, and
as it were DEIFIED.1029
‘Reduction to the simplicity of the Monad,’ discussed in more detail in the fol-
lowing chapter on Khunrath’s Cabala, analogous to the reduction of matter
to its primal state in the alchemical laboratory, is the way that fallen man, the
Old Adam, can be regenerated and returned to a prelapsarian state. Circular
Figure 2 of Adam-Androgyne could, perhaps, be argued to stand for both
Adam before the Fall and the desired state of being ‘reinstated in integrity’.
Writing of an early modern ‘spiritual alchemy of rebirth’, Mike Zuber identi-
fies Khunrath as ‘among the very first to explicitly posit an analogy between
the Incarnation of Christ, the preparation of the philosophers’ stone and the
rebirth (regeneratio) of the individual believer’.1030 Carter Lindberg states that
while orthodoxy regarded rebirth as one element within the ordo salutis, it is
the central motif for Pietism, which understood it as comprising the entire pro-
cess of salvation.1031
