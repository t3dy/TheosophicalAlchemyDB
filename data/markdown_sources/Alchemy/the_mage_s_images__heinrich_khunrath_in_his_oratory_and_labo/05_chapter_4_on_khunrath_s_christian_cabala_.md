climaxing in a form of mystical identification with Similitudo (Likeness).

37 1) Fides (Faith)
The Amphitheatre’s ten-step ascent begins with the fundamental requirement
of Faith, as Khunrath states at the top of Circular Figure 2:
It will be fitting for Us, therefore, to offer ourselves first piously washed
and penitently purified, and, on bended knee, to commend ourselves,
with sincere faith, to the Deific Light.751
The honey of Divine Wisdom, we are told, is prepared ‘from the flower of your
Mind’, that is, from ‘Faith, Love, Hope and Invocation’,752 with the first of these

Elsewhere in the same work Reuchlin adds that ‘through faith our mind is con-

By Faith, Khunrath does not mean the simple performance of external prac-

wisdom, industry, riches and gifts demonstrated by many.767 The two vices
of infidels are ἀπιστια [apistia – unbelief] and audacity or rashness towards

cles, the impediment to obtaining ‘great and admirable things’ from God and

sublimation or exaltation.’795
