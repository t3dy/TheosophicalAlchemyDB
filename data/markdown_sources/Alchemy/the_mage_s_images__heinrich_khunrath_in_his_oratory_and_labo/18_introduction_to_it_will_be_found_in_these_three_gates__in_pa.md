A voice issuing from a fire must surely have held great resonance to an
alchemist! Khunrath gives some indication of how he understands these
names by similarly discussing היהא Ehieh and the phrase spoken by God to
Moses in Exodus 3:14 ‘Ehieh eser Ehieh, I will be he who will be’.603 He explains
that this name refers to the past, present, and future, with the implication that
it is eternally significant, as implied in the Amphitheatre’s title, and he relates
it to John’s ‘ἀπὸ τοῦ ὁ ὢν, καὶ ὁ ἦν, καὶ ὁ ἐρχόμενος, from him THAT IS, and THAT
WAS and THAT IS TO COME’ from Revelation 1:4.604 There is thus the impli-
cation that this Old Testament name, Ehieh, anticipates the future coming,
namely the return of the Messiah and is an example of how Khunrath, like Pico
and Reuchlin before him, sought to use their knowledge of Hebrew to make
‘Cabalistic’ readings of the Old Testament as ‘proof’ of the eternal existence of
all three persons of the Holy Trinity, thereby refuting, for example, the heresies
of Arius and the Antitrinitarians, and, in theory, converting the Jews.605

33 9. הושהי YHSVH, Pentagrammaton, Verbum Mirificum
In the context of Cabala and occult philosophy, the cruciform posture of
Khunrath’s Christ (without the presence of the Cross), within a circle, calls to
mind Agrippa’s human figures in geometric shapes in Book 2 of De Occulta
Philosophia (1533, Fig. 4.49). Agrippa’s microcosmic man as the measure of the
universe becomes Khunrath’s Christ at the centre of creation.
The ten divine names appear in a radiant pentagram of fire, comprised of
five major flames along with five sets of eight smaller flames, forty-five in total.
One possible meaning for this is an allusion, perhaps, to pseudo-Dionysius’s
forty-five names of God.634 With the proliferation of Hebrew, there is another
possibility: in standard Gematria, called Mispar Hechrachi or Ragil, following
the regular numeric attributions of the Hebrew letters, 45 is the value of the
Hebrew word םדא Adam (א = 1 + ד = 4 + ם = 40). In the method of Gematria
