## Section: Conclusions, Pico demonstrates his detailed knowledge of the Jewish system.

Conclusions, Pico demonstrates his detailed knowledge of the Jewish system.
He writes that the Cabalist can communicate with God through the
archangels in an ecstasy that may result in the death of the body, a way of
dying known as the “Death of the Kiss” (Cabalistic Conclusion 11). He sets
out a table which shows the correspondences between the ten spheres of the
cosmos and the ten sephiroth (Cabalistic Conclusion 48). He also describes
the states of the soul in relation to the meanings of the ten sephiroth (such as
unity, intellect, reason) and traditionally links the highest sephiroth with the
lowest in a circular arrangement (Cabalistic Conclusion 66).6
