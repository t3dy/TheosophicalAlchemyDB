About the Author
JAY KINNEY is coauthor of Hidden Wisdom: A Guide to the Western
Inner Traditions. For fifteen years, he was publisher and editor-in-chief of
Gnosis, the premier journal covering esoteric traditions and spiritual paths.
In addition, Kinney is a member of Mill Valley Lodge #356 and Mission
Lodge #169, F&AM, in California; a member of the York Rite; and a 32°
KCCH in the Scottish Rite. He has twice been a speaker at the California
Masonic Symposium, and is a recipient of the Albert G. Mackey Award for
Excellence in Masonic Research. He has extensive contacts within
Freemasonry and, as librarian and director of research for the San Francisco
Scottish Rite, has access to many resources and Masonic records that have
eluded most popular writers on this topic.
