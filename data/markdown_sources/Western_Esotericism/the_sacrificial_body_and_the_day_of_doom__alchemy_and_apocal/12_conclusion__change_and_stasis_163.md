Alchemical allegory was composed of images involving the death
of two beings, male and female, that were purged of their impuri-
ties and eventually raised again to life in the form of a totally different
singularity, a miraculous entity that was the Microcosmic Christ,
both material and spiritual in their essence. The philosopher’s stone
transcended death and brought heaven itself down to earth. It was
the alchemical equivalent of the New Jerusalem, a talisman against
the disease and corruption of a dark and uncertain world.
