knowledge and power of the magi, there are four indispensable conditions—an
intelligence illuminated by study, an intrepidity which nothing can check, a will
which cannot be broken, and a prudence which nothing can corrupt and noth-
ing intoxicate. TO KNOW, TO DARE, TO WILL, TO KEEP SILENCE—such are the four
words of the magus, inscribed upon the four symbolical forms of the sphinx.”
Lévi, Transcendental Magic (1896), 30.
