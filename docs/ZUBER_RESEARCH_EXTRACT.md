## Part 2: Spiritual vs. Material Alchemy

### Historical Development
Zuber traces how alchemy evolved from primarily material (laboratory operations) to spiritual (interior transformation) across the 16th-18th centuries.

**Timeline from Zuber:**
- 16th c: Laboratory alchemy (Paracelsus, operational focus)
- 17th c: Transition (Cramer, Maier—both operational AND spiritual)
- 18th c: Spiritual emphasis (Böhme lineage, Swedenborg, mystical interpretation)

**Key Distinction:**
- Material alchemy: "Making gold" (though understood symbolically by the wise)
- Spiritual alchemy: Actual interior transformation of the practitioner

**Research Notes:**
- Extract Zuber's explanation of this evolution
- Extract how same emblems/texts were read differently in material vs. spiritual contexts
- Extract how practitioners integrated both dimensions
- Extract what "true alchemy" meant to different figures

**Implications for Phase 2:**
- Concept definitions must show BOTH material and spiritual meanings
- Emblems illustrate this duality (what they show operationally ≠ what they mean spiritually)
- Figure biographies show which figures emphasized which dimension
- Historical development section in concept essays
