**Key Themes to Extract:**
- Embodied practice emphasis (theory must connect to actual doing)
- Spiritual/interior alchemy vs. material alchemy distinction
- Transmission genealogy: Böhme → Swedenborg → Blavatsky → modern esotericism

- Integration of mystical experience with philosophical rigor

**Where This Informs Phase 2:**

- Concepts like "Inner Transformation," "Theurgic Practice," "Embodied Knowledge" need grounding in actual operations
- Figures like Jacob Böhme, Mary Anne Atwood, Jane Lead get enriched with practice emphasis
- Emblems illustrate actual operations, not abstract philosophy

**Extraction Strategy:**
- Identify passages on spiritual vs. material alchemy distinction
- Extract quotes about women practitioners
- Note transmission lineages with dates/figures
