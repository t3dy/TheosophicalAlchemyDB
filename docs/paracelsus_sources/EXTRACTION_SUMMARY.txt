MAJOR SCHOLARLY DEBATES (7 central)

1. Luther or Faust? (Reformer vs. charlatan)
   - Weeks: Autonomous anomaly; neither/nor
   - Current consensus: Responds to authority crisis

2. Religious vs. Scientific Interpretation (False dichotomy)
   - Weeks: "Theory" bypasses division
   - Consensus: Fundamentally synthetic thought

3. Influences and Genealogy (Multiple traditions)
   - Medieval Spiritualism, Neoplatonism, Hermeticism, Gnosticism,
     Alchemical tradition, Hildegard influence, practical craft knowledge
   - Status: All plausible, none definitive

4. Luther-Paracelsus Parallel (Contextually important, not doctrinally)
   - Both respond to 1520s crisis of authority
   - Both reformers in respective domains
   - Both assert independent authority
   - Parallel contextual, not direct influence

5. Chronology of Key Writings (Concentrated in 1520s-30s)
   - 1524-1525: Earliest theological polemics (Salzburg)
   - c.1527-1528: Basel year as intellectual peak
   - 1530s-1541: Consolidation and late theology
   - Thesis: Originated in crisis moment, not timeless philosophy

6. Textual Authenticity (Post-mortem editions complex)
   - Died 1541; scattered manuscripts; centuries of editorial work
   - Posthumous editions by disciples, some apocryphal
   - Authentication crucial for interpretation
   - Weeks approach: Text-centered, careful attribution

7. Alchemy's Role (Recent trend: central, not peripheral)
   - Traditional: Practical knowledge incorporated into medicine
   - Sparling 2020: Transmutational alchemy as central philosophy
   - Recognition: Alchemy integrates theory-praxis, magic-medicine

HISTORIOGRAPHICAL INNOVATIONS

Weeks's Text-Centered Historicism:
  - Approach Paracelsus as writings in historical context
  - Evaluate by internal coherence, not anachronistic standards
  - Avoid claiming him as prophet for our time
  - Let historical context emerge from texts themselves

Weeks's "Theory" (Theorica) Concept:
  - Bypasses false science/religion dichotomy
  - Encompasses medical, philosophical, theological, mystical speculation
  - Neutral with regard to empiricism vs. metaphysics
  - Allows integration of speculation-observation, theory-practice

Crisis of Authority Framework:
  - 1520s Reformation not just theological but intellectual upheaval
  - Affects medicine, philosophy, epistemology across disciplines
  - Paracelsus's work as response to this crisis
  - Parallel institutional challenges: church vs. universities
