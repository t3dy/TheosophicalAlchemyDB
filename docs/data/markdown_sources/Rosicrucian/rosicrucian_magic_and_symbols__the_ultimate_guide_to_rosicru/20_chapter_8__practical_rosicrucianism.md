The Rosicrucian Order has been in existence for over four centuries, but its
beliefs are not widely taught except among the more secret orders of
Freemasonry. The “practical” origins of the Rosicrucians are seen in their
community outreach programs through various hospitals, clinics, and
institutions for those with mental illness. The Rosicrucian philosophy also
theorizes that every human possesses a divine essence called the “inner
god,” and through meditative practices, you can not only better understand
the divine but improve all aspects of life.
