Donne, Herbert, and Vaughan facilitates the deliberate forgetting of the

Whereas Donne conjoins alchemical with Eucharistic imagery to provide some

uses alchemy to signal how he fi nds such questions serious but unanswerable.
Tracking Herbert closely, Vaughan—q uite surprisingly, considering that he

m atter entirely. Vaughan’s physical world is not a world of physics, and it is
alchemy that signals it so.
