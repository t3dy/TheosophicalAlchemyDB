From the time of the early Church Fathers, the study of the Mosaic account
of Creation in Genesis had been regarded as a necessary introduction to the
understanding of the universe.493 Similar to Giovanni Pico della Mirandola
in the Heptaplus (1489), Khunrath takes the book of Genesis as the founda-
tion for the Christian-Cabalist work of Bereshit, the wisdom of nature, with its
author, ‘Moses, Truest historian of all Nature’ as the primary authority.494 The
second set of Khunrath’s authorities belong to Ficino’s prisca theologia or prisci
sapienti, the tradition of those who though not Christian showed evidence of
sympathetic teachings, as possible foreshadowers of Christianity, the ancient
theology or its representatives, the ancient wise, including Hermes, Orpheus,
Pythagoras, and Zoroaster.495
The Hermetic Pimander provided a description of creation that comple-
mented the account in Genesis and the Emerald Tablet of Hermes Trismegistus
was understood as teaching of the correspondences interconnecting all lev-

cal tradition of ancient philosophy, by which Khunrath presumably intends
works like Plato’s Timaeus, Iamblichus’s De Mysteriis, and Plotinus’s Enneads

have the Physico-Chemical philosophers, with Paracelsians like Gérard Dorn
