Notes on Contributors
Frances Andrews
Ph.D. 1994 London, is professor of mediaeval history at the University of St An-
drews. Her publications include The Other Friars (Woodbridge, 2006), Church-
men and Urban Government in Late Medieval Italy (Cambridge, 2013) and The
Church and Doubt, co-edited (Cambridge, 2016).
Valeria De Fraja
Ph.D. (1999), Università cattolica del Sacro Cuore Milano, is an independent
scholar and she worked at Istituto Storico Italiano per il Medio Evo—Rome
(2007–2013) and at Verona University. She has published monographs (Oltre
Cîteaux. Gioacchino da Fiore e l’ordine florense, Roma 2006) and she has ed-
ited critical editions of Joachim of Fiore’s and Maio of Bari’s texts.
Alfredo Gatto
Ph.D. (2013), San Raffaele University, is postdoctoral researcher at the Univer-
sity of São Paulo. He has published articles and monographs on divine om-
nipotence, with particular regards to Peter Damian, and he edited the Italian
edition of Joachim’s De septem sigillis (Mimesis, 2013).
Peter Gemeinhardt
Dr. theol. (2001), University of Marburg, is Professor of Church History at the
University of Göttingen. His research focuses on education and religion, trini-
tarian theology, and hagiography. Recent publications include Antonius: Der
erste Mönch. Leben—Lehre—Legende (C.H. Beck, 2013).
Sven Grosse
Dr. theol. (1993), Dr. theol. habil. (1999), University of Erlangen (Germany) is
Professor for Historical and Systematic Theology at Staatsunabhängige The-
ologische Hochschule Basel. He has published monographs and essays, includ-
ing Ich glaube an die Eine Kirche. Eine ökumenische Ekklesiologie (Schöningh,
2015).
Massimo Iiritano
Ph.D. in philosophy of religion (2001), has been a lecturer in anthropology of
religions at the University for Foreigners of Perugia, research assistant at the
chair of philosophy of religion at the University of Siena-Arezzo and at the
chair of aesthetics at the University of Perugia. Recent volumes include Picture
