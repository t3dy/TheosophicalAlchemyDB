prelapsarian, Edenic state, in which Adam and Eve heard the voice of God walk-

The idea of deification (with its cognates: divinization, theosis, apotheosis and
exaltation), of making someone or something divine or of becoming divine,
reaches far back into antiquity and can be found discussed in many of the
sources used by Khunrath. As David Litwa makes clear, ‘there is no single form
of deification … but the many types are united by a set of interlocking themes:
achieving immortality, wielding superhuman power, being filled with super-
natural knowledge or love – and through these means transcending normal
human (or at least “earthly”) nature.’1037 For Saint Paul deification, or rather

By reading the Amphitheatre’s three ‘books’ in the sequence ‘In US, in NATURE,
in SCRIPTURE’, the Theosopher first seeks to know his own soul, which as
microcosm ultimately embodies or reflects all things, and then, studies, loves,
and cares for humanity and the world at large, purging and purifying himself
in the process, emulating and loving God, such that he eventually unites with
God to become like Christ, a divine man.1077 Khunrath’s conception of the
Theosopher’s deification recalls the Ficinian position in which man is con-
ceived of not only as the image of God, but as potentially ‘a god on earth and a
god in the world to come’.1078
Khunrath’s ultimate vision is that of total purification of the microcosm
(body, spirit and soul), in order for the ‘righteous Spirit’ to enter into man’s ‘inner-
most parts.’1079 After the example of Solomon, one should seek the removal of
vanity and lies from the soul and moderation of fortune for the body.1080 The
