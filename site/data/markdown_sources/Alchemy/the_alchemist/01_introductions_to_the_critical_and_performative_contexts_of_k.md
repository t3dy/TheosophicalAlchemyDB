introductions to the critical and performative contexts of key Elizabethan
and Jacobean plays. Each guide not only introduces the text’s critical and
performance history but also provides students with an invaluable insight
into the landscape of current scholarly research through a keynote essay on
the state of the art and newly commissioned essays of fresh research from
different critical perspectives.
