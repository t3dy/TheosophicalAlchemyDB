Welcome to TheosophicalAlchemyDB

What This Portal Is

TheosophicalAlchemyDB is a comprehensive interactive knowledge portal documenting the history, philosophy, and significance of Rosicrucian and spiritual alchemy traditions from the 16th to 19th centuries. It contains 386 scholarly entities including historical figures, alchemical concepts, primary texts, thematic essays, and emblems from foundational emblem books (Maier's Atalanta Fugiens, Stolcius's Hermetic Garden, and Cramer's Rosicrucian Emblems).

How to Navigate

Main Navigation: Use the buttons at the top to move between sections: Figures (100 historical persons), Dictionary (67 alchemical and mystical concepts), Texts (83 scholarly sources), Essays (thematic scholarly articles), Emblems (135 emblem plates), Emblem Books (grouped emblem collections), Map (geographic distribution), and About (this page).

How to Search and Filter

Global Search: Type in the search box at the top to find entries across all sections simultaneously. Use the checkboxes below the search box to limit results to specific entity types (Figures, Dictionary, Texts, Emblems).

Section Filters: Within each section, use the filter bars to sort and refine:

  • Figures: Sort alphabetically or chronologically, filter by nationality, scholar, or century
  • Dictionary: Sort A→Z, filter by category (alchemical operations, hermetic principles, etc.)
  • Texts: Sort by date or title, filter by language or century
  • Emblems: Sort by title or publication order, filter by source book (Atalanta Fugiens, Hermetic Garden, Rosicrucian Emblems) or type
  • Emblem Books: Filter by source book and thematic focus (Nigredo, Albedo, Rubedo, Hermetic Principles, Rosy Cross)

How to Read Full Essays

Each card displays a 2-4 sentence summary. Click any card to open the full essay in a modal window, which includes extended scholarly apparatus, related concepts and figures (clickable for navigation), historiographical debates, and source citations. Use the back button to navigate through related entities.

How to Use the Map

Geographic Distribution: The map shows locations associated with figures (red), publication sites of texts (blue), emblem book origins (purple), and major learning centers (orange). Use the legend to toggle layers on and off. Click any marker to see details in the side panel, including the entity's name, dates, role, and scholarly significance. All entities are geographically grounded in historically documented locations.

About the Emblem Gallery

The Emblems section displays all 135 emblem plates from three foundational works: Maier's Atalanta Fugiens (1617, 51 emblems), Stolcius's Hermetic Garden (1624, 40 emblems), and Cramer's Rosicrucian Emblems (1617, 44 emblems). Each emblem includes the plate image (where available from digitized sources), alchemical significance, related concepts, and scholarly interpretation. The Emblem Books section organizes these by source book, allowing browsing by publication order or thematic focus (the three stages of the Great Work: Nigredo, Albedo, and Rubedo).

Scholarly Framework

This portal integrates scholarship from key historians including Frances Yates (Renaissance magic and Rosicrucianism), Joscelyn Godwin (theosophical traditions), Tobias Churton (Rosicrucian history), Urszula Szulakowska (alchemical emblems), and Mike Zuber's definitive work on spiritual alchemy from Jacob Böhme to Mary Anne Atwood. Historiographical rigor is maintained throughout: scholarly debates are presented fairly, not resolved; evidence gaps are acknowledged; legends are distinguished from documented facts. All entities include source citations enabling further research.

Relational Browsing & Discovery

Every entity links to related concepts, figures, and texts. Click any hyperlinked name within an essay to navigate directly to that entity. The back button returns you to your previous location. This design enables serendipitous discovery: start with a figure and explore the concepts they developed, then follow those concepts to other thinkers and primary texts.

Technology

Built with vanilla JavaScript, Leaflet.js for interactive mapping, and a comprehensive JSON database. All filtering, sorting, search, and relational navigation run client-side for instant responsiveness. The portal remains fully functional offline once loaded.
