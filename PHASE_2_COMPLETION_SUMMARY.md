- Art-historical: Maier's integration of operational and philosophical dimensions
- Alchemical: Red sulfur immersion in the final working stage
- Philosophical: The king's surrender to transformative power
- Spiritual: Ego dissolution in divine consciousness
- Influence: Swedenborg studied this emblem; later Rosicrucians reinterpreted

### Example 2: Concept Enrichment (Zuber Lens)

**Nigredo (Concept 1):**
- Operational: "Materials putrefy, decompose, lose original form through heat application"
- Philosophical: "Principle of dissolution—breaking down false certainties and ego structures"
- Spiritual: "Death of false self, essential for genuine transformation. Practitioners understand as breakdown necessary for restructuring consciousness"
- Transmission: Traced through alchemical tradition from Jabir ibn Hayyan through Renaissance alchemists to Swedenborg

### Example 3: Figure Enrichment (Akerman Lens)

**Johann Valentin Andreae (Figure 1):**
- **Historiographical Debate:**
  - Yates argues: Created literary Rosicrucian fiction inspiring real mystical movements
  - Vickers argues: Explicitly opposed Rosicrucian claims, distanced himself from movement
  - Recent scholarship: Used Rosicrucianism as pedagogical tool, later rejected literal interpretation
- **Gender Awareness:** "As Protestant theologian, inherited scholastic frameworks excluding women from formal intellectual participation"
- **Transmission:** Documented how ideas spread from Andreae through Fludd, Maier to Swedenborg
